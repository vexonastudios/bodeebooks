## Fitness illustration prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape image for the BodeeFit homeschool fitness panel on a family website. Create a warm, polished editorial storybook illustration of two school-age siblings happily doing simple standing stretches on a grassy backyard lawn, with leafy trees and a modest home softly suggested in the background. Wide 3:2 composition, both children fully visible near the middle, clear silhouettes readable at small card size. Natural everyday modest athletic clothes and sneakers. Soft morning sunlight; sage and forest greens, warm cream, muted terracotta and gold, subtle painted texture. Welcoming, active, not babyish. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no border. This will accompany classic book cover art and colorful game art on a cream-and-green website.
